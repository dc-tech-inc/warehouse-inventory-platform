# 5. UI direction: a ledger, not a dashboard of cards

Status: accepted

**Context.** The people using this book stock all day, often at a desk next to a dock. They need dense, scannable tables and fast forms, not a marketing look.

**Decision.**

- *Feeling:* dense as a tally sheet, calm as a ledger. Table cells use 8 px × 12 px padding on a 4 px grid; numbers are tabular and right-aligned.
- *Palette from the physical warehouse:* paper manifest background, steel blue for actions, hazard-tape yellow and forklift orange for attention. One accent for actions; yellow and orange only mean "look here".
- *Signature:* rows at or below their reorder point carry a hazard-tape stripe on the left edge, and the overview is a ruled tally strip of five numbers instead of icon cards.
- *Depth:* borders only, low-opacity rules, no shadows. Inputs sit slightly darker than their surface.
- *Every view has loading, empty and error states;* focus rings stay visible, controls are at least 44 px high on touch devices, motion is under 200 ms and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; light and dark both work.
- *No component library:* native `<select>`, `<details>` and `<table>` cover this app's needs and keep the keyboard behaviour for free.

**Consequences.** The look is specific to this product and cheap to maintain (one CSS file of tokens). The screenshots in the README are generated by the e2e tour, so they cannot drift from the app.
